Quick start

How do I dictate my first sentence?

Allow microphone access and place your cursor in a text field. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space to start, speak, then press it again to insert your words. On Wayland, use the floating microphone and paste the result from the clipboard.

Where do I find E Voice after installation?

On Windows, E Voice starts in the system tray. Double-click the desktop shortcut to open Settings. On macOS, the installer opens the app and adds a desktop shortcut. On Linux, open E Voice from the application menu after installing the DEB.

How do I bring the microphone to my cursor on Windows?

Open Settings and enable “Вызывать тройным щелчком” (triple-click). Quickly click the left mouse button three times in one spot outside E Voice. The floating microphone appears beside the pointer.

Which languages can I dictate in?

E Voice recognises Russian and English with the included GigaAM v3 model. You can speak either language without changing a setting. Recognition is strongest in Russian. Check longer English passages for errors.
